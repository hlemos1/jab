# StrikeX visual identity · October 2026

This interface follows the supplied StrikeX Brand Book v1 and existing StrikeX franchise dossier. The business functionality remains at Stage 6.

## Assets and direction

- `public/brand/strikex-logo.png`: original embedded logo from the supplied Brand Book; used without recoloring, cropping or distortion.
- `arena.webp`, `reflex.webp`, `immersion.webp`: original embedded imagery from the supplied StrikeX digital franchise dossier. These are brand concepts, not proof of installed equipment or live telemetry.
- Black #0B0B0B, graphite #171717, bone white #F7F1E8, Strike Red #E5292F. Functional buttons use #DD252B for white-text contrast. Cyan communicates ready/connection states; station colors remain distinct.
- Barlow Condensed Bold supports the condensed athletic headline direction. Self-hosted original font from `google/fonts`, `ofl/barlowcondensed/BarlowCondensed-Bold.ttf`, blob `9e480f467d703009e3abfce290b2ec2b7d24e188`. The accompanying SIL OFL is distributed alongside it. Body text uses system Arial/Helvetica.

## Scope

Official logo, photographic arena hero, CombatFit concept cards, navigation branding, shared tokens, typography, forms, class planning, coach panels, reports, units, account and rewards. Existing training callbacks, APIs, data and Solver rules are unchanged. The arena now also exposes a direct link to unit classes.

The full Brand Book and franchise dossier are not bundled or published with the app.
